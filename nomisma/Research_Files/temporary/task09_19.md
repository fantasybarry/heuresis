# Task 09_19 — proposed priorities and simulation improvements

Date: September 19, 2026

Status: **Proposal exported; implementation and new experiments pending.**

This plan follows the [loss investigation](task09_17_loss_diagnosis.md).
Its immediate priority is to verify the price-increment and entry-execution
rules, then evaluate entry extension and exit distances on broader data with
an untouched evaluation period. Improvements should make the simulation
more faithful and its conclusions more reliable; increased profit is not an
acceptance criterion for a simulator correction.

## Requirements and existing baseline

- Initial capital: **$1,000**, cash account.
- Risk requirement: **$100 maximum total account drawdown**, modeled as a
  fixed-dollar threshold below the running equity peak, without daily reset.
  Preserve reporting of delayed liquidation, unknown equity and overshoot;
  the simulation cannot establish a guaranteed loss ceiling.
- Primary allocation policy: at most one entry episode per day, with the
  maximum quantity permitted by settled cash, remaining risk and liquidity.
- Frozen strategy baseline: **1.5 ATR stop, 2× reward multiple, 15-minute
  maximum hold**, with current confirmation and selection rules.
- Existing settlement accounting, reservations, infeasible-order rejection,
  partial fills, drawdown controls, cost/delay stresses and reproducibility
  checks remain the starting point. These capabilities are already implemented.

The latest nominal single-entry result was −$18.70 from one FTFT trade,
using $247.60. All registered comparisons traded only one symbol-date.
The earlier development leader recorded in task09_18.md remains an exposed
research result, not an accepted replacement or an untouched validation case.

## Priority 1 — verify prices and the execution contract

**Why first:** 27 of 71 alerts had an impossible entry interval after rounding.
The engine correctly rejected them; the underlying assumptions need review.

- [ ] Identify the intended execution adapter and applicable order-price
  precision by instrument, order type, price level and historical date.
  Distinguish allowed order prices from quote/trade precision and fill prices.
- [ ] Specify whether entry is a locally triggered IOC limit order or a
  broker-native conditional order, which event triggers it, and when routing
  begins. Keep the existing synthetic behavior as an explicit baseline.
- [ ] Separate the submitted price limit, execution-price displacement and
  fees. A modeled buy fill must respect its price cap; fees are a separate
  cash debit. Preserve an explicit rejection for a genuinely empty interval.
- [ ] Verify boundary cases around $1, low ATR, equal trigger/cap, sub-cent
  data, partial fills and quote jumps. Do not silently widen caps or reduce
  displacement to force executions.
- [ ] Reconcile each of the 27 rejected alerts under the documented contract.
  Classify any changed outcome as a justified model correction or a separate
  strategy experiment.

**Deliverable:** Versioned price/execution specification, conformance tests,
and a before/after rejection audit. Completion requires internally consistent,
documented rules—not more fills or higher P&L.

Current reference: Alpaca documents up to two decimals for limit prices at or
above $1 and four below $1. This is broker order-entry precision, not a universal
tick rule for all data or confirmation of the user's broker. Apply only after
establishing the adapter and date-specific rules.
[Alpaca order documentation](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/orders-at-alpaca).

## Priority 2 — improve diagnostics and execution realism

- [ ] Add a timeline for each alert/order: initial and latest prefill score
  components, recent returns, extension above VWAP, ATR/price, signal age,
  price levels, requested quantity, binding sizing cap, and rejection or fill
  reason. Include unfilled and blocked opportunities without inventing fills.
- [ ] Preserve event, modeled receipt, decision, submission, activation and
  fill timestamps separately. Label unknown original receipt times explicitly.
- [ ] Model fill-report and cancellation-acknowledgment timing separately from
  exchange events. Distinguish actual simulated holdings from what the strategy
  knows, cover fill/cancel races, and reconcile reservations after acknowledgments.
  Zero fill-report latency is a current assumption, not a measured property.
- [ ] Report quoted mark-to-market equity separately from a capacity- and
  delay-constrained liquidation scenario, including any unfilled remainder.
  A displayed quote does not establish immediately executable value for the
  entire position. Specify the risk observer before changing its inputs.
- [ ] Audit partial-fill capacity across successive quote updates, including
  repeated sizes and uncertain replenishment. Use explicit conservative
  scenarios where depth or queue evidence is unavailable.
- [ ] Separate bar, trade, quote, routing and fill-report delay sensitivities.
  The existing doubled-timing stress changes several delays together; isolated
  scenarios would make attribution clearer. Preserve the combined stress too.
- [ ] Expand targeted scenarios for stale quotes, gaps, missing minutes,
  halts/resumptions, delayed cancellations and incomplete exits. Missing
  observations remain unknown; OHLC bars must not manufacture quote fills.
- [ ] Carry daily cash/settlement state across all sessions, including
  no-trade dates; validate broker posting assumptions when evidence is available.

**Deliverable:** Per-order diagnostic records, execution-assumption report and
targeted regression/stress results. Reconcile cash and shares at every event,
respect price limits, retain unresolved positions, and reproduce identical
outputs for the same inputs. More conservative modeling may reduce reported
profit or execution frequency.

## Priority 3 — register and expand the evidence

- [ ] Freeze the eligible historical universe, development/validation/holdout
  periods, acquisition budget, missing-data policy and decision criteria before
  inspecting additional outcomes. Use information available at stock selection.
- [ ] Screen minute coverage and corporate-action consistency first, then
  acquire the required quotes/trades for qualifying coverage. Keep records of
  excluded symbols and sessions to expose selection bias.
- [ ] Collect prospective market-data receipt and clock-quality evidence
  without submitting orders. Estimate supported delay ranges; retain uncertainty
  where the actual broker/execution path is unobserved.
- [ ] Obtain observations across independent symbol-dates and market conditions,
  including cases where cash or risk actually limits allocation. Fifty-share
  results cannot simply be multiplied to represent deploying all $1,000.
- [ ] Predefine the required estimation precision and handling of dependence
  across trades, symbols and days. Determine evidence sufficiency from those
  requirements rather than choosing an arbitrary passing trade count.

**Deliverable:** A registered protocol and versioned dataset with provenance,
coverage/clock reports and an untouched chronological holdout.

## Priority 4 — test entry and exit hypotheses

This stage depends on a defensible execution model and broader evidence.

- [ ] Study whether recent price extension and distance above VWAP are related
  to subsequent favorable/adverse movement, conditioning on information known
  at the decision. Keep quote-based excursion descriptive, not an executable
  exit at the retrospectively best price.
- [ ] Evaluate whether target distance and stop distance fit observed volatility
  and the holding horizon. A 2:1 payoff ratio does not estimate hit probability.
- [ ] Register a small, bounded comparison of baseline versus selected entry
  or exit hypotheses—for example, a contemporaneous extension filter or an
  explicitly defined profit-protection exit. Choose definitions and ranges
  before evaluation; do not fit them to this trade's $5.23 peak.
- [ ] Treat prefill signal revalidation and quality-based symbol selection as
  strategy changes. The losing trade's last prefill signal was still eligible,
  so its loss does not establish stale-signal failure.
- [ ] Keep the user's single-entry maximum-allocation policy as the primary
  case. Retain the existing multiple-entry result as a labeled comparator;
  its one-day advantage does not justify changing the policy.
- [ ] Attribute changes individually, then test the specified combination.
  Record all trials, costs, exposure, drawdown, unknown intervals, nonfills and
  performance by date/symbol, rather than selecting solely by total profit.
- [ ] Freeze one candidate only if development criteria are met, evaluate it
  once on the untouched holdout, and retain rejection or insufficient evidence
  as valid outcomes. A failed holdout must not become a repeated tuning target.

**Deliverable:** A documented acceptance, rejection or insufficient-evidence
decision. No new numerical parameter values are accepted in this proposal.
