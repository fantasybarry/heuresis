## Data and baseline assumptions

The archive contains **265 provider response pages**, **7,999 cohort minute
bars** (including extended hours), **956,191 quote records**, and **1,500,713
trade records** across 12 symbol/session combinations. The normalized dataset
is approximately 300 MB.

Data quality remains explicit: 15 missing regular-session minutes, 366 crossed
quotes, one invalid quote price and 16 out-of-bounds bar records. Invalid quote
states remain timeline invalidations. Importantly, **4,124 distinct observations
with reused trade IDs were preserved**, rather than mistakenly discarded.

The fixed planner uses a 1.5× minute-ATR stop, 2× reward multiple and 15-minute
holding limit. The testing account starts with $10,000; risk fixtures are$25
per trade, $100 daily and$75 simultaneous scenario risk, with $1,000 maximum
position notional and$3,000 gross exposure. Fees and adverse displacement are
$0.001/share each as specified. Observation and routing delays are one second
each; fill-report delay is zero. These are engineering assumptions, not the
user's account, risk preferences or verified broker execution costs.

### Why the old results cannot be read as parameter quality

The frozen $10,000 baseline had 37 orders; **28 had an empty admissible ask
interval** after slippage. For example, a trigger and cap of $4.49 with $0.001
slippage requires an ask between $4.49 and $4.489. All 22 TE orders in that
baseline were structurally unfillable. This was retained and audited, not
silently repaired in the frozen model.

The issue matters at $1,000: on September 14 at 15:42 UTC, an impossible TE order
reserved **$992.366**, leaving **$2.184**, and blocked a later FTFT opportunity.
The passive $1,000 reference ended at $988.70.
Its fills, cash ledger, orders, trades and summary match the guarded baseline;
31 decision records differ only in account context.
Any improvement over the $10,000 pilot must not be attributed to the guard
without this capital control. A revised entry-feasibility rule requires a
separate registered model version and new evidence.

## Update — 2026-09-18: cash-account settlement and single-stock allocation

The user clarified that the $1,000 account is a cash account and authorized
testing one selected stock with the largest allocation permitted by settled
cash, remaining fixed $100 peak-drawdown headroom and modeled liquidity.
The earlier task content and experiments above are preserved as written.

### Implemented and executed

- Added a separate cash-account replay; the earlier replay/risk modules and
  app remain byte-identical. The old replay immediately reused sale proceeds.
  Its baseline's three purchases nevertheless totaled only $957.90 including
  entry fees, so that trade count alone did not demonstrate improper recycling.
- Track settled cash, pending reservations and signed unsettled proceeds.
  Sale proceeds remain in equity but cannot fund another intraday round trip.
  Negative exit-fee proceeds remain liabilities. Settlement and cancellation
  do not invent profits or reduce equity merely by moving cash between balances.
- Use archived provider settlement dates, including Labor Day/weekends, with
  modeled availability at 09:30 Eastern on T+1. Unsupported dates fail closed;
  missing replay dates do not postpone settlement. Actual broker posting time
  is still unknown.
- Reject structurally impossible entries before creating orders/reservations.
  Entry caps, fees, displacement, signal confirmations and price inputs were
  not relaxed to generate fills.
- B/C use the first feasible qualifying alert, with alphabetical same-time
  ties, one pending order and one positive entry episode per date. An expired
  unfilled order releases the choice; partial IOC fills consume it. No later
  entry uses leftover cash that day. Carried holdings block entries while open.
- C removes the prior artificial $25/$75 allocation caps and fixed notional
  ceilings as binding constraints. The $100 running-peak drawdown control,
  cash funding and liquidity limits still apply. No stop was tightened simply
  to increase allocation.
- Froze stop 1.5 ATR, reward 2 and hold 15 minutes. Executed three nominal
  variants and four separate B/C cost/timing stresses across all nine exposed
  development dates, plus an exact C repeat. No parameter search occurred.
